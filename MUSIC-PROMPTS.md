# Optional music directions

These are prompts for generating original instrumental music; no music track is currently added to the scene. Choose one of the two suggested genres for each generation. My first choice for the island is prompt 1: organic ambient, leaving the existing nature ambience in the foreground.

## 1. Floating Sakura

Genre alternatives: **organic ambient / Japanese ambient**.

```text
Original instrumental music for a floating sakura forest at night, with fireflies, translucent water and a distant pastel cosmic horizon. Organic ambient with a Japanese ambient sensibility: sparse felt-piano notes, delicate koto-like harmonics, breathy soft pads and occasional glassy overtones. A slow, free-floating pulse around 60 BPM, gentle suspended and added-ninth harmonies, tender and quietly mysterious. Spacious arrangement, restrained dynamics, long natural decays. Music only, leaving room for separately mixed insects, water and owl calls. Beatless, vocal-free, loop-friendly, about two minutes, with a soft entrance and exit.
```

## 2. Stardust Drift

Genre alternatives: **space ambient / ambient downtempo**.

```text
Original instrumental soundtrack for a peaceful cosmic garden: a small living island beneath stars, lilac blossoms and a distant gravitational halo. Space ambient or very restrained ambient downtempo, around 62 BPM. Warm evolving analogue pads, airy granular textures, sparse rounded bell tones and a light, soft bass foundation. Slowly opening harmonies, a sense of wonder, distance and weightlessness. For downtempo, use only a barely perceptible brushed pulse; keep the ambient version beatless. Gentle continuous dynamics, generous silence between phrases, vocal-free, music-only, seamless-loop character, about two minutes.
```

## 3. Firefly Letters

Genre alternatives: **lo-fi ambient / gentle chillhop**.

```text
Original instrumental music for watching glowing fireflies trace symbols above a quiet night forest. Dreamy lo-fi ambient or gentle chillhop, 68–72 BPM. Soft Rhodes chords, a few felt-piano notes, muted guitar harmonics and very subtle tape warmth. A simple memorable two- or three-note motif, warm suspended chords, rounded bass, intimate and unhurried. In the chillhop version, use sparse brushed percussion with a relaxed swing; in the ambient version, let the harmony carry the pulse. Keep the arrangement light enough for nature ambience to remain audible. Vocal-free, music-only, understated transitions, loop-friendly, about two minutes.
```

## Mixing direction

Treat music as an optional quiet layer with its own volume and toggle. Keep the nature recordings as the main sense of place. Test both a long listening session and a full firefly performance before choosing a track. The nature and water recordings are already supplied by the scene; the generated music should not duplicate them.
